# Session Notes

The DM's session companion: the in-game clock and calendar, sessions and notes, the party's deaths and Favor of Fate, weather by place and climate, and optional systems each campaign can switch on in Settings:

- **Disease tracking with stages** and **survival conditions** (hunger, thirst, fatigue, temperature), in the style of Giffyglyph's Darker Dungeons
- **Random outbreaks** (homebrew)
- **Downtime training** and a **magic item broker** (table rules)

With all of them off, the app follows the rules as written. Each campaign keeps its own calendar (Settings, Calendar), so any setting's months, weekdays, moons and holidays work. Nothing setting-specific is built in: your campaigns, places and disease library sync privately through your Google Drive.

Part of The DM's Perspective suite (cruise105.github.io): the party comes from the Character Sheet app, and what players should see reaches their sheets live.
