Treated with magic

Darker Dungeons: magic doesn't purge a disease at once, it sends it into decline, if the magic is strong enough for the disease's DC.

Disease

DC 5–10: most people shake it off. 15–20: needs bed rest or medicine. 25–30: almost no one recovers. Incubation is also how often it can get worse.

Effects add up: stage 3 has stages 1 to 3. Writing "gain thirst at twice the normal rate" (or hunger, or fatigue) makes the survival tracker double it.

What each stage does on the character sheet

Normally read from the stage's own text ("-2 DEX and -2 WIS", "disadvantage on all rolls"). To change it, write it here the same way; leave a box empty to go back to reading the stage.

Character

For features like a paladin's Divine Health, enter the class level. Darker Dungeons: levels 1–5 resist up to DC 15, 6–10 up to DC 20, 11–15 up to DC 25, 16+ every disease.
